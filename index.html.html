Forms of Assessment & Implementation Matrix

Practical guidelines on how to conduct CBC/TVET assessments effectively without overburdening learners or teachers.

Assessment FOR Learning

1. Formative Assessment

Ongoing evaluation conducted during the learning process to provide immediate feedback and adjust instruction.

Practical Implementation Methods:
  • Exit Slips / Muddiest Point: Learners write 1 key takeaway and 1 question before leaving class.
  • Thumbs Up / Traffic Light: Instant visual checks for understanding during direct instruction.
  • Peer Assessment Checklists: Trainees check peer practical procedures against step-by-step rubrics.

KICD/TVET Target: Daily classroom feedback, non-graded diagnostic tracking.

Assessment OF Learning

2. Summative Assessment

Evaluates learner achievement at the end of an instructional unit or term against benchmark standards.

Practical Implementation Methods:
  • End-of-Topic Performance Task: Standardized practical tasks or written tests.
  • CDACC Institutional Assessment: Modular practical examinations in laboratory/workshop settings.
  • KICD Grade 9 / Senior School Assessments: Standardized school-based assessment projects.

KICD/TVET Target: Summative reporting, certification, termly evaluation.

Pre-Instruction Assessment

3. Diagnostic Assessment

Identifies learners' prior knowledge, skill gaps, and misconceptions before introducing new content.

Practical Implementation Methods:
  • KWL Charts (Know, Want to Know, Learned): Filled at topic initiation.
  • Pre-Topic Diagnostic Quizzes: Short 5-question baseline tests to group learners by ability.
  • Safety & Tool Identification Inventory: Used in TVET laboratories before equipment access.

KICD/TVET Target: Differentiating instruction, catering to LLN requirements.

Real-World Competency

4. Authentic / Portfolio Assessment

Requires learners to apply skills to real-world tasks and curate evidence of ongoing competence over time.

Practical Implementation Methods:
  • Learner Portfolio Files: Curated best work, photos of practical items, and reflective logs.
  • Community Service Learning (CSL) Projects: Real community problem-solving projects.
  • Laboratory Calibration Prototypes: Operating flame photometers or executing chemical synthesis.

KICD/TVET Target: CSL verification, CDACC practical evidence portfolios.

Step-by-Step Implementation Framework for Teachers

Step 1: Unpack Learning Outcomes

Extract Learning Outcomes & Benchmarks from the unit standard and determine whether knowledge, skill, or attitude is tested.

Step 2: Select Tool

Choose between a 4-level descriptive rubric, a checklist, or oral questioning/written test checks.

Step 3: Execute & Give Feedback

Assess learners live during class. Give immediate actionable oral/written feedback instead of just marks.

Step 4: Record & Remediate

Log ratings into assessment logs. Plan targeted remediation for learners needing support.
